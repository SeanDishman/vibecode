Fishing Tycoon

Sal fishes. You don't. He casts, waits, hooks and winds in on his own, all day, through squalls and thunderstorms — and every fish he lands is money in your pocket. Watch the sky: storms drive strange things up out of the deep, and when the rain clears to gold the sunfish come up to bask. Both pay far better than anything that swims in fair weather. Your entire job is the research tree — a net over the side for a steady trickle, then longer rods for deeper water, better bait, stronger line and reel power, and eventually a whole crew of rods over the side. Drag and zoom around it like a map: eight tracks, seventy rungs, and every rung is a named piece of kit rather than another notch on a curve — every third reel head is geared, every third line is a braid. Everything you buy is bolted onto the boat where you can see it.
